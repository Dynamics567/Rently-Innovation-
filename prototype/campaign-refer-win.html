How it works

Four steps from sign-up to score.

01

Become an Ambassador

Create a Rently account (or use your existing one) and accept the Ambassador agreement. You get a personal referral code instantly.

02

Share your link

Send your referral link to Rental Partners you know — on WhatsApp, social, wherever. Every signup through it is tracked to you automatically.

03

They onboard, you score

As each referral registers, gets verified, and lists a verified listing, points land on your ledger — never for a click alone, only for real progress.

04

Climb the leaderboard

Your score updates live. When the challenge closes, the highest-scoring Ambassador is confirmed as the winner by Rently.
